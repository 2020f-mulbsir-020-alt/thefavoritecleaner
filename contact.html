Before We Arrive

Helpful Preparation Checklist

  • Clear counters and floors of excess clutter where possible
  • Secure cash, jewelry, and fragile valuables
  • Confirm access (keys, codes, gate, or an adult present)
  • Share pet details and any safety notes in advance
  • Note parking, building rules, allergies, or product preferences
  • List priority rooms or focus areas for the visit
  • Leave a short note if you will not be home — we work carefully either way
